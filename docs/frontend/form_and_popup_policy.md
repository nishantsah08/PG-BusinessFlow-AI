# Form And Popup Policy

## Purpose
Defines the application-wide rule for forms and modal popups used across authenticated portal screens.

## Policy
- Use a form layout with labels on the left and controls on the right.
- Prefer dropdowns when values are bounded and known in advance.
- Prefer search-select controls for system entities such as leads, staff, units, properties, and vendors.
- Use free-text inputs only when no controlled source exists.
- When a form asks for proof, attachment, receipt, screenshot, or any other artifact, provide an upload button with multi-file support instead of a raw link-only input.
- Show a short read-only preview before submitting workflow-backed actions.
- Keep popup language operational and business-readable, not tool-shaped.

## Scope
This policy applies across modules such as:
- HR
- CRM
- Property & Booking
- Finance
- Workflow and approval surfaces
